About Me

I'm a developer who builds complete systems — from hardware to app.

I'm a software developer based in South Korea, studying at Daedeok Software Meister High School, a specialized technical high school focused on software engineering.

My experience spans embedded systems, edge AI, robotics, and application development. I like starting from a real problem and building the whole system that solves it — writing firmware, running models on-device, wiring up the backend, and shipping the app that ties it together.

Right now I'm especially interested in gaining experience in international development environments and turning designs into fast, clean, production-ready web.
